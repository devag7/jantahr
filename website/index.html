Every deadline, on one calendar.

Salary and TDS by the 7th, EPF and ESI by the 15th, quarterly returns, bonus and full and final settlements, each ticked off with its challan number.

Payroll in minutes.

Mid-month joiners, loss of pay from attendance, arrears and loans, then payslips, bank advice and the EPF return from the same run.

Self-service on any phone.

Everything HR runs on.

One product from offer letter to full and final settlement, with the statutory work done for you.

  • Payroll and TDS

    Formula-driven salary structures, proration, LOP, arrears, loans, old and new tax regimes with projection, Form 16 and payslips.

  • PF, ESI, PT, LWF

    EPF with the ₹25,000 ceiling, ESI coverage rules, state professional tax including half-yearly states, labour welfare fund, ECR and challan files.

  • Labour codes

    Wages under the 50% rule, statutory bonus, gratuity and fixed-term employment as notified from November 2025.

  • Leave and attendance

    Policies with accrual, carry-forward and encashment, shifts and rosters, geo-fenced and selfie check-in, biometric device push.

  • Onboarding and exits

    Checklists, resignation workflow, clearances, full and final settlement and relieving letters.

  • Performance and hiring

    Appraisal cycles with self and manager reviews, calibration, salary revision, careers page, pipeline and one-click hire.

  • DPDP privacy

    Consent ledger, data export, correction and erasure requests with the 90-day window, breach register and retention-aware anonymisation.

  • Secure by default

    Supabase Auth with two-factor, field-level encryption for PAN, Aadhaar and bank details, audit log, tenant isolation and row-level security.
