# Code of conduct

JantaHR follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Maintainers may remove content and block participants who do not follow this code.
