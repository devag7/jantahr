# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employees and managers of Indian companies with 10 to 500 people, on Android phones (often mid-range, sometimes on
patchy mobile data), between tasks: checking in at the office gate, applying for leave on the way home, opening a
payslip on salary day, approving a request between meetings. HR and payroll admins use the website for heavy work.

## Product Purpose

JantaHR runs HR and payroll for Indian companies (PF, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS, leave, attendance, self-service). The phone
app is the employee and manager front door: check in, see and apply leave, read payslips, file expenses, approve
requests. Success: the daily action takes seconds, and the app feels as trustworthy as a banking app because it holds
salaries and personal data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, dependable. Quiet confidence rather than playfulness: the user should feel "this is a serious product
my company chose", like Apple Wallet or Stripe, with warmth in a few moments (a greeting, a finished check-in, an
approval sent). Plain, friendly Indian English; numbers in Indian format (₹1,53,079).

## Anti-references

- Clip-art people, cartoon mascots, childish icons (rejected in the logo round).
- Generic HRMS look: dense grey tables, many colours, stock photos of handshakes.
- Neon or crypto-dark styling; heavy gradients everywhere; glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

1. One action per screen leads (check in, apply, approve), and it sits in the thumb zone.
2. Brand colour in a few committed places (the Home hero, the sign-in, the splash); everywhere else restrained.
3. Numbers are the hero: balances, hours and pay are large and tabular; labels are small.
4. Delight only at completions: check-in, leave applied, request approved.
5. Respect slow phones and reduced motion: state changes under 250 ms, no page-load choreography.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast, 48 dp touch targets, TalkBack labels on every control, content that survives 160% font scale.
